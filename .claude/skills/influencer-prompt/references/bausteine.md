# Prompt-Bausteine (Englisch, zum Kombinieren)

Regel: pro Prompt **eine** Fotogattung, **eine** Hauptlichtquelle, **ein** Kamera-Setup, **1–2** Imperfektionen. Widersprüchliche Stile (z. B. „film photo“ + „iPhone selfie“) machen das Bild künstlich.

## A. Fotogattung (Satzanfang)
- `Candid smartphone photo of` – Standard für Feed
- `Front-camera selfie of` – Stories, „Talk to camera“-Feeling
- `Mirror selfie of` – Outfit-Posts
- `Casual photo taken by a friend of` – Lifestyle, Urlaub, Café
- `Photo from a phone on a small tripod, self-timer, of` – Home, Sport
- `Flash photo at night taken with a phone of` – Party, Abend, Restaurant (Direktblitz: harte Schatten, rote Augenreflexe vermeiden: "no red eye")
- `35mm film snapshot of` – nur für bewusste „Analog-Serie“, nicht mischen
- `POV shot, her hands visible, no face:` – Füllposts

## B. Kamera
| Setup | Text |
|---|---|
| iPhone 1x | `shot on a phone main camera, ~26mm equivalent, f/1.8, natural perspective, moderate depth of field, slight computational HDR` |
| iPhone 2x / Portrait | `phone 2x telephoto, ~52mm equivalent, portrait mode with soft computational bokeh, slight blur errors around loose hair strands` |
| Frontkamera | `front camera, ~23mm wide, arm's-length distance, slight wide-angle stretch near frame edges` |
| Ultraweit | `0.5x ultrawide phone lens, strong perspective, slight edge distortion` (nur für Räume/Gruppen) |
| Kamera (Freund mit DSLR) | `35mm lens, f/2.8, eye level, natural proportions, no extreme wide-angle distortion` |
| Porträt edel | `85mm portrait lens, f/2, sharp focus on the eyes, soft background bokeh` |
Perspektive ergänzen: `eye level` / `slightly from above (selfie angle)` / `low angle from table height` / `from across the room`.

## C. Licht (genau eins als Hauptlicht)
- Fenster: `soft window light from camera-left, gentle falloff, natural shadows, realistic catchlights in the eyes`
- Bewölkt draußen: `overcast daylight, soft even shadows, neutral white balance, realistic contrast`
- Goldene Stunde: `low golden-hour sun from behind her, warm rim light on hair, slight lens flare, face in soft shade`
- Mittagssonne (ehrlich): `harsh midday sun, short hard shadows, slight squint` (wirkt sehr echt)
- Innen abends: `warm 2700K lamp light from a floor lamp on the right, rest of the room dim, slight sensor noise in the shadows`
- Bildschirm/Handy: `face lit by phone screen glow, blue-ish cast, dark room`
- Blitz: `direct on-camera flash, hard shadow behind her on the wall, slightly overexposed foreground, dark background`
- Gemischtes Licht (Café): `daylight from the window mixed with warm tungsten ceiling lights, natural colour mix`

## D. Haut, Haare, Hände
- Close: `natural unretouched skin, subtle pores at close focus, slight redness around the nose, fine vellus hair, restrained highlights`
- Medium: `natural skin texture, no beauty filter, no airbrushing`
- Haare: `a few flyaway hairs catching the light, natural uneven parting, hair slightly frizzy from the rain`
- Hände: `relaxed hands, five fingers each, natural knuckles and nails` – Hände groß im Bild oder ganz raus, nie winzig.
- Make-up: `minimal everyday makeup, slightly smudged mascara at the end of the day` / `no makeup`

## E. Kleidung & Stoffe
Immer Material + Zustand: `oversized grey cotton hoodie with slightly stretched cuffs`, `light-wash denim with natural fading at the knees`, `linen shirt with creases from sitting`, `knitted wool sweater with a few pills`. Keine Markenlogos: `plain, no logos`.

## F. Orte (lebendig machen)
2–3 Requisiten, die „gelebt“ wirken: `half-empty glass of water, charging cable on the bed, a jacket thrown over the chair`, `receipt and keys on the café table`, `slightly messy kitchen counter with a cutting board and herbs`. Hintergrund-Personen: `a few other guests in the background, out of focus, not looking at the camera`.

## G. Farbe / Nachbearbeitung
- Standard: `natural colours, true-to-life white balance, slight phone sharpening, no filter`
- Leicht warm: `slightly warm, soft contrast, like a lightly edited Instagram photo`
- Story: `straight out of camera, unedited`

## H. Ausschlüsse (kurz halten)
`no text, no logos, no watermark, no waxy or airbrushed skin, no extra fingers, no distorted face`
Bei Bildbearbeitung stattdessen Erhaltungssatz: `Edit only [X]. Keep her face, facial proportions, freckles, expression, crop and background unchanged. Do not slim the face, enlarge the eyes or smooth the skin.`

## I. Verbotene Wörter (machen KI-Look)
ultra realistic, hyperrealistic, 8k, 4k, masterpiece, best quality, highly detailed, perfect skin, flawless, CGI, render, octane, unreal engine, cinematic (außer bewusst Film-Look), studio lighting (außer Studio-Shooting), beautiful/gorgeous (beschreibe stattdessen konkrete Merkmale).
